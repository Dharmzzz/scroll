1955 – 1979 • The Foundations

Madras Affiliation to Kalamassery Campus
🖉Why 1955 – 1979? (Historical Reason & Significance)

Founded in 1955 under the CMI congregation with the Department of Social Work affiliated to Madras University, transitioning to Kerala University in 1960, relocating to the permanent Kalamassery hilltop campus in 1967, and inaugurating the first live research & NSS training laboratories in 1972–1973.

RCSS Independence, OYSTER & Global MOUs
